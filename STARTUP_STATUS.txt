⚙️  CONFIGURATION REQUIRED

Before the application is fully functional, you need to configure:

1. Azure AD / M365 Authentication
   File: backend/.env
   Required: AZURE_CLIENT_ID, AZURE_CLIENT_SECRET, AZURE_TENANT_ID
   Source: https://portal.azure.com

2. CardDAV / Nextcloud Integration
   File: backend/.env
   Required: CARDDAV_URL, CARDDAV_USERNAME, CARDDAV_PASSWORD
   Source: Your Nextcloud Admin Panel
